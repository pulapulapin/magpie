Questions

Is it free?

You can use every feature for 14 days. After that, a single purchase unlocks it, with all updates in version 1.x included. There is no subscription.

Which edition should I pick?

If you need BitTorrent, FTP or the browser extension, take the direct edition. If you only keep ordinary videos, audio and articles, the App Store edition is the easiest.

What am I allowed to save?

That depends on who owns the content and on the terms of the site it comes from. Magpie only saves a copy on your own computer. It does not remove copy protection and cannot tell whether you are allowed to save a given file. Your own material, openly licensed material and content you are authorised to use are the safe cases. Please follow each site’s terms and the law where you live.

Does it upload my files?

No. Everything stays on your Mac. There is no account and no analytics.

What does it need?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 12 or later.

Can I install both editions? Are purchases shared?

Yes, they install side by side (the direct edition is called Magpie Direct). They are separate products: each has its own 14-day trial, its own history and its own purchase. A purchase in one does not unlock the other, and trial extension codes only work in the direct edition.

Where do I report a bug?

On the support page. It takes a minute.
