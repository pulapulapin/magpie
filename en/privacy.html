Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

In one sentence

Magpie does its work on your Mac. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We do not collect, receive or upload anything you save. On the App Store, the privacy label for Magpie is “Data Not Collected”.

Where your data lives

DataWhereNotes
Saved videos, audio, articles, filesThe folder you choose (default: Downloads/Magpie)Stays on your Mac
Library records, settings, progressMagpie’s application data folderStays on your Mac
Website sign-insThe app window’s own website dataStays on your Mac. Clear it any time in Help

When Magpie uses the internet

Only because of something you do:

  • You save a link: Magpie connects to the site you gave it and to that site’s media servers.
  • You buy or restore the purchase (App Store edition): the purchase is handled by Apple.
  • Direct edition only: if you press “Check for updates”, it contacts the yt-dlp release page on GitHub; BitTorrent links connect to the DHT and trackers, and other peers can see your IP address; optional speech models are downloaded only after you confirm.

Magpie does not send anything to our servers. We do not run any.

Purchases and licences

App Store edition: payment, receipts and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; Magpie only learns whether the purchase is valid. Direct edition: licence keys are verified on your Mac with a public key built into the app; activating a key does not contact any server.

Website sign-ins

For sites that need you to sign in, you sign in yourself in the app window. Magpie never reads or stores your password. It uses the signed-in state only to fetch what you ask it to save.

Clipboard

Clipboard notices are off by default. If you turn them on, Magpie reads only links, to recognise them, and keeps nothing else.

Your choices

  • Clear website sign-ins in Help at any time.
  • Delete Magpie’s application data folder to remove all records.
  • The files you save are yours. Delete them whenever you like.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions or bug reports: GitHub Issues.
