Magpie Support

Everything you need to use Magpie, and how to report a bug.

Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
  • macOS 12 or later.
  • An internet connection to save links. Saved files work offline.

Getting started

  1. Open Magpie. Your 14-day free trial starts with every feature on.
  2. Copy a link, then press “+” at the bottom left and paste it. (Or turn on clipboard notices in Help and just copy a link.)
  3. Choose video, audio or article, and the quality. Press Save.
  4. Files go to Downloads/Magpie. The first time, macOS may ask for folder access: choose Allow.
  5. To change where files go, open Help → Files and space → Change.

Questions

The trial ended. What now?

Open Help → Authorization and press “Unlock full version”. Your saved files and history stay available either way.

I bought it already.

Open Help → Authorization and press “Restore purchase”. Use the same Apple Account.

Where are my files?

In Downloads/Magpie, in folders for video, audio, articles and files. Help → Files and space shows the exact path.

A video won’t play in QuickTime.

Open the item and choose “Convert”. Magpie makes a version your Mac can play, or you can open the original in another player.

Can I save from a site that needs me to sign in?

Sign in once in the app window. Magpie never sees your password and keeps you signed in. You can clear this in Help.

Why was my link refused?

Magpie tells you the reason in plain words. Common ones: the stream is protected, the page is private, or the site isn’t supported in this edition.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. See the privacy policy.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases are refunded by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

When something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
“Interrupted”, or a download stoppedPress Retry. Magpie resumes where it can. Check your internet connection and free disk space.
“No permission” or the folder can’t be writtenHelp → Files and space → Change, and choose a folder in your user folder or on an external disk.
The link is recognised but nothing is foundThe site may have changed or the page is private. Try opening the page in the app window, or report it below.
Conversion failsFree some disk space and try again. If it keeps failing, report it and include the file type.
Everything looks broken after an updateQuit and reopen Magpie. Your files and history are never touched by an update.

Report a bug

This is the only support channel. Please include:

  • What you did, what you expected, what happened.
  • Your macOS version and the Magpie version (Help → Download engine and diagnostics).
  • Help → Download engine and diagnostics → “Copy diagnostics” and paste the result. Links, emails and keys are blanked out automatically.
